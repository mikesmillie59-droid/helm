FIRST-TIME SETUP

Set up My Helm charts

My Helm does not include a shared LINZ key. Each boat owner needs an access key issued to their own LINZ Data Service account.

  1. Get or view your LINZ access key. Keep the key private.
  2. Copy the key from LINZ and paste it below.
  3. Select Save key and use LINZ charts. Charts need internet unless you save an area under Settings → Offline charts.

You can reopen this guide or change the key later in Settings → Advanced & troubleshooting. LINZ keys are never included in Saved Places backups.

Voice & demo

Speaks a warning when your current course will take you into a saved hazard’s circle within this time. Works once the boat is moving at about 1.5 knots or more. The dashed line on the map shows where you’ll be.

Offline charts

Save LINZ charts on this device so they still show with no mobile signal. First close Settings and move the map so the screen shows the area you want. Everything on screen is saved, down to each chart’s full detail.

Checking saved charts…

Offline copies don’t update themselves, and LINZ corrects charts over time. Delete and download again every few months. The street map and OpenSeaMap buoys are not saved offline.
